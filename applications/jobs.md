# Language and travel jobs: search of 3 Oct 2026 (Indeed)

Filter: fully remote, **or** the employer pays you to travel or go on ships. Languages: Japanese and English first, then Hindi, Marathi, Telugu and Kannada.

**Reality check.** Almost every Japanese↔English *interpreting* job asks for JLPT **N1** (some N2) plus 2–3 years of interpreting experience. With NAT-TEST Level 3 (about N3), you'll get the best results from jobs where **engineering is the main skill and languages are a bonus**. You can also aim for Hindi interpreting once you reach N2.

## Ways to unlock more of these jobs
1. **Pass JLPT N2.** It unlocks Hindi↔Japanese medical and community interpreting, which is rare and well paid at up to ¥9,000/hr. Next JLPT: first Sunday of December 2026 (registration has likely closed) or July 2027.
2. **Get a Cisco CCNA.** It qualifies you for cruise-ship IT officer contracts: months at sea with cabin and food included.
