## What only you can do

- **ISOC Fellowship:** create an Internet Society account and enrol in the Pre-Fellowship Training. Finish it by 22 Nov. You also need to record a video of up to 3 minutes; a script is in the draft.
- **Every form:** press Submit and tick the declarations yourself. I don't submit or sign anything on your behalf.
- **Visas:** Canada (ICANN89) needs a visitor visa for Indian passport holders. Start as soon as you're selected.
