## Why do you want to become an ICANN Fellow? (~200 words)

I have built web systems for six years, and I have depended on the DNS the whole time without taking part in how it is governed. At TCS I built microservices for an international airline's crew operations, where a resolution failure means a dashboard that operations staff can't load. Today I design caching and signed-URL strategies for a cloud document viewer, where every request depends on names and certificates resolving correctly. I understand the engineering side well. I understand far less about how policy on registries, DNS abuse and universal acceptance gets made, or who makes it.

I also work across borders. I'm Indian, I live and work in Japan, and I've worked on projects for Mercedes-Benz R&D India, an international airline and companies in Japan such as Kawasaki Heavy Industries. From that position I've seen how unevenly the Internet works for people who use different scripts and languages. That's why universal acceptance of internationalized domain names and email addresses interests me in particular.

I'm applying as a working engineer who wants to learn the process properly and then contribute to it, not as someone who already has the answers.
